Good questions

      

        How much does a cleaning cost?
        
Every space is different, so main services are quoted individually after we see the size and condition. Stainless steel appliance exteriors have a set price guide (above), and your final quote is always confirmed before any work begins.

      

      

        How do I pay?
        
Send a booking request here, we confirm your quote, and then we send you the payment details. Booking is by email, and no payment is collected on this page.

      

      

        How do Holiday Perks work?
        
Turn on Holiday Perks in the booking estimator or just mention them when you call. Your special holiday discount is applied when we confirm your quote. Our newest fliers also list Referral Rewards and Loyalty Perks for Returning Clients — ask about those when you book, too.

      

      

        Do you offer a military discount?
        
Yes — thank you for your service! With the Military Discount, deep cleaning is $75–$140 and no estimate is needed. Just choose “Military Discount Deep Cleaning” in the booking form or mention it when you call.

      

      

        What is the School Kids Room Cleaning Discount?
        
From August through July every year, school kids’ rooms get a deep cleaning for $75–$100 — no estimate needed. Pick “School Kids Room Cleaning” in the booking form any time of the school year.

      

      

        How much is trash can cleaning?
        
Residential is $25 per can and commercial is $50 per can. Bundle deals save you more: 2 residential cans for $45 (save $5), 3 for $65 (save $10), and 4 or more at $20 each. We clean on-site, sanitize, and leave your can fresh — no dumpsters.

      

      

        Do you clean dumpsters?
        
No — trash can cleaning is for cans only, not dumpsters. It’s great for homes, businesses, restaurants, HOA communities, apartment complexes, and event venues.

      

      

        Do you offer dryer vent cleaning?
        
Yes — we clear built-up lint from the filter and vent so your dryer runs better and safer. You can see a before & after in the Our Work section above.

      

      

        How does the College Student Discount work?
        
College students get a deep cleaning for $75–$100 with no estimate needed. Appointments must be scheduled 1 week in advance, and reschedule & cancellation fees will apply.

      

      

        What does the HRT Building deep cleaning include?
        
High dusting and low dusting throughout the facility, bathrooms scrubbed and sanitized, and paper towel dispensers cleaned inside & out — plus the detail work in between. You can watch clips from the job in the HRT Building section above.

      

      

        What areas do you cover?
        
We serve the 757 area — homes, offices, schools, medical centers, restaurants, churches, and more. Call 757-977-4743 if you’re not sure whether we reach you.
